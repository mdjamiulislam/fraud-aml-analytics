RISK TIER METHODOLOGY

Risk tiers are based on model-score percentiles from the validation period.

Critical = top 0.5% of validation scores.
High = 99th to 99.5th percentile.
Medium = 95th to 99th percentile.
Low = below the 95th percentile.

These are relative model-risk tiers, not calibrated probabilities.

The operational investigation threshold remains the validation-F2 threshold selected in Step 10.

MODEL GOVERNANCE NOTES

1. Risk-tier thresholds were developed using validation scores only.

2. The final test period was not used to define tier thresholds or the operational alert threshold.

3. HGB model scores are ranking scores, not calibrated real-world fraud probabilities.

4. The investigator-facing queue excludes the actual fraud label because that value would not be known in live operations.

5. The evaluation queue retains isFraud only to measure model performance in this synthetic portfolio project.

6. Risk tiers help prioritisation; the separate operational threshold determines which transactions are formally alerted.
