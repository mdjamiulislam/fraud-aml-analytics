MODEL GOVERNANCE NOTES

1. The final test period must remain untouched during model development.
2. Any imputation, scaling, encoding, or class balancing must be fitted only on training data.
3. Post-transaction balances and isFlaggedFraud are excluded from the primary model to reduce leakage risk.
4. Accuracy is not an appropriate primary metric because fraud is highly imbalanced.
5. Threshold selection must use validation data, not the final test data.
